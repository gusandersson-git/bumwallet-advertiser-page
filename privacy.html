Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

BumWallet is operated by Loot Forge Media ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect when you add the BumWallet pass to your wallet and use our offers, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Account details you give us: email address and, at cashout, the identity details we need to verify you are 18 or over and in the United States.
  • Device details: a device identifier, wallet pass serial number, device model, operating system, IP address and approximate location from that IP.
  • Activity: offers you view, click and complete, and the balance and payout history on your pass.

Why we collect it

  • To credit your balance when an advertiser confirms you completed an offer.
  • To pay you and to confirm you are eligible to be paid.
  • To prevent fraud, including one account per device and location checks.
  • To keep the pass and the offers working and to answer support requests.

Who we share it with

Offer partners and advertising networks receive a device identifier and completion data so they can confirm conversions. Payment providers receive what they need to send your payout. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep account and completion records for as long as your account is active and for [number] years afterwards, so we can handle disputes and reversals.

Your choices

You can remove the BumWallet pass from your wallet at any time. To delete your account and data, email gus@lootforgemedia.com from the address on the account.
