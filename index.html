Format

BumWallet is a pass in Apple Wallet and Google Wallet, not a store app. Ad click to pass added is one landing page and one tap. The pass links into the offerwall, updates the balance on your postback, and posts new offers to the lock screen. No store install drop off, and nothing to uninstall, so retention runs higher than a typical rewards app.

Users

US, 18 and over, daily mobile gamers on real devices. Marketed as a few dollars a day for games they already play, not as income, which keeps expectations low and completion rates steady. Best fit for game installs with level goals, then app trials and sign ups, surveys as filler, and purchase or deposit offers once an account has a clean history.

Fraud and payouts

Fraud controls

  • One account per device, enforced with device fingerprinting and wallet pass serial numbers
  • Geo and VPN checks at signup and at every offer click, US only
  • Emulator and rooted device detection before an offer is shown
  • Credit is applied only after your postback confirms the conversion, never on click
  • Velocity limits on completions per account per day
  • Manual review of the first payout on every account

Payout policy

  • Minimum cashout threshold of $5.00
  • Identity check at first cashout
  • Earnings are held until the conversion clears your reversal window, so reversals land on us, not on you
  • Public terms of service and privacy policy, linked from the pass and the site
  • Support inbox answered by a named person, not a bot
  • Accounts that trigger a reversal are frozen and reviewed before any further offers
